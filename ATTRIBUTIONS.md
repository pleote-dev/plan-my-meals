# Image & Recipe Attributions

All starter recipe visuals shipped in `custom_components/plan_my_meals/frontend/img/*.svg` are **100% original vector artwork** created specifically for this project and released under the **MIT License** (see `LICENSE`).

No third-party copyrighted photographs from food blogs, cookbooks, or commercial recipe platforms are distributed in this repository.

When users upload their own photos or download images via `plan_my_meals.upload_image`, those files are stored locally in the user's own Home Assistant instance (`/config/www/plan_my_meals_img/`) and are never committed to this repository.
